Privacy notice

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who runs this site

Beyond Redundancy is an independent site run by an individual in the UK. You can reach me through the contact form.

What the contact form collects

Only what you type: your message, and your name and email address if you choose to give them. The name and email are optional. If you don’t give an email address, I can read your message but can’t reply.

How it is sent

The form uses a third-party service, Web3Forms, which passes your message on to me by email. Web3Forms says it keeps submissions for 30 days on its free plan, and it may process data outside the UK. I use your message only to read it and, if you gave an email address and asked for a reply, to answer you. I don’t add anyone to a mailing list, and I don’t share or sell what you send.

How long I keep it

I keep messages only for as long as I need them to deal with them, and then delete them.

The rest of the site

  • No cookies or analytics. The site doesn’t currently use cookies or visitor tracking. If that changes, I will update this page first.
  • The budget planner works entirely in your browser. What you type into it isn’t saved or sent anywhere.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit. That is covered by GitHub’s own privacy statement.

Your rights

You can ask me what information I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to how I use it, by sending a message through the contact form. If you are unhappy with how I have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
