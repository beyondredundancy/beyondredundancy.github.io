About this site

I’ve just been made redundant after more than 20 years in manufacturing, and I’m working out where my own money should go. This site is where I write down what I find, so the next person doesn’t have to start from scratch.

I’m not a financial adviser. I draft the guides with the help of AI tools, then check every figure against official sources such as GOV.UK before publishing, and I show the date each guide was last checked.

Information, not advice. These guides explain how things work in general. They are not personal financial advice, and rules and rates change. Check the official source linked in each guide, or speak to a regulated adviser, before you decide.

Official sources: GOV.UK and MoneyHelper.
