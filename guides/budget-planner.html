Free redundancy budget planner

Last checked: 25 October 2026

How it works

Enter your monthly figures in pounds. The planner shows how much your essential outgoings exceed your income, and how many months your cash would last. Everything is worked out in your browser. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.

Income you will still have each month

For example a partner’s pay, benefits or part-time work. Leave it at 0 if you have none.

Essential outgoings each month

Bills you can’t drop. Tip: look back through 12 months of bank and card statements so you don’t miss anything, especially annual bills such as car tax, MOT, insurance renewals and your TV licence. For annual bills, divide the cost by 12.

Other spending each month
Cash you can reach

Money in accounts you can get at without a penalty.

To think about how many months of cover to aim for, read our guide to how much cash to keep accessible. If you might claim benefits, see our guide to benefits while job hunting. If your outgoings are higher than you would like, see our guide to cutting your monthly outgoings.

Information, not advice. This planner is a simple tool. It doesn’t include irregular bills, tax, or changes to your income, so treat the result as a starting point.
